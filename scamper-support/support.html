🚨 Emergency Situations

If you are experiencing an outdoor emergency, DO NOT rely on this app for help.

  • Call 911 immediately (or your local emergency number)
  • Have someone call for help while you assist the injured person
  • Do not attempt to move someone with potential spinal injuries
  • Always inform someone of your outdoor plans before heading out

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find nature spots near me?

Open the app and allow location access when prompted. Use the Map view to see nearby trees, rocks, waterfalls, and caves. Use the Search tab to find spots based on specific criteria like type, difficulty, features, or distance from your location.

How do I add a new nature spot to the app?

Tap the "+" button and select what you want to add (Tree, Rock, Waterfall, or Cave). You'll need to provide the location, photos, difficulty rating, type/subtype, and any special features. Make sure you have permission to share locations on private property.

Are these outdoor activities safe?

Outdoor activities like tree climbing, rock climbing, cave exploration, and waterfall visits carry inherent risks and should only be attempted by those with proper training, equipment, and experience. Always follow safety guidelines, use appropriate gear, and participate within your skill level. See our Safety Guidelines for detailed information.

Can I access nature spots on private property?

Never access nature spots on private property without explicit permission from the landowner. Always respect private property rights and follow local laws and regulations.

How are difficulty ratings determined?

Difficulty ratings are based on factors like tree height, branch accessibility, technical climbing requirements, and overall challenge level. Ratings range from 1 (beginner-friendly) to 5 (expert level). These are community-driven ratings, so your experience may vary.

Why isn't the app finding my location?

Make sure you've granted location permissions to Scampr in your device settings. If you're still having issues, try restarting the app or your device. Some indoor locations may have poor GPS signal.

How do I report inappropriate content?

If you see content that violates our community guidelines, please contact us at jordangillispie@outlook.com with details about the content and why you believe it should be removed.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes, you can delete your account through the app settings. This will remove your personal information, but trees and reviews you've shared may remain to preserve community content. Contact support if you need complete data removal.

The app is crashing or not working properly

Try these troubleshooting steps:

  • Force close and restart the app
  • Check for app updates in your app store
  • Restart your device
  • Make sure you have a stable internet connection
  • Free up device storage space

If problems persist, contact support with your device details.

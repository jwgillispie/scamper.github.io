Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 26, 2024

Introduction

At Scampr, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect several types of information from and about users of our App:

Data Type What We Collect Purpose
Account Information Email address, display name, authentication credentials Account creation, authentication, communication
Tree Content Tree locations, descriptions, photos, difficulty ratings, features App functionality, sharing with community
Reviews & Ratings Your reviews, ratings, and comments about trees Community features, tree information quality
Location Data GPS coordinates (when you use location features) Finding nearby trees, location-based search
Usage Analytics App interactions, features used, time spent Improving app performance and user experience
Device Information Device type, OS version, app version, crash reports Technical support, bug fixes, compatibility

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide Core Services: Enable tree discovery, mapping, and community features
  • Location Services: Show nearby trees and calculate distances for search results
  • Community Features: Allow users to share tree information and reviews
  • Safety: Provide tree safety information and climbing guidelines
  • Communication: Send important app updates, safety alerts, and support messages
  • Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to enhance app functionality
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraudulent or harmful activities
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with applicable laws and regulations

Location Data Handling

Scampr uses location data to provide core functionality:

  • Tree Discovery: Finding trees near your current location
  • Search Enhancement: Prioritizing search results by proximity
  • Map Features: Displaying your location on the interactive map
  • Tree Creation: Adding location information when you submit new trees

Location Control: You can disable location services in your device settings. Some features may be limited without location access.

Sharing of Information

We may share information we collect in the following circumstances:

Public Information

  • Tree Locations: Tree coordinates and information you share are visible to all users
  • Reviews & Ratings: Your reviews and ratings are public with your display name
  • User Profiles: Your display name and climbing statistics may be visible to others

Service Providers

  • Cloud hosting and database services
  • Analytics and crash reporting services
  • Email and notification services
  • Map and location services

Legal Requirements

  • To comply with legal obligations or court orders
  • To protect safety, rights, or property
  • To investigate potential violations of our Terms
  • In connection with business transfers or mergers

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption: Data transmission and storage encryption
  • Access Control: Limited access to personal data
  • Authentication: Secure user authentication systems
  • Monitoring: Regular security monitoring and audits
  • Updates: Regular security updates and patches

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for different periods based on the type of data:

  • Account Data: Until you delete your account
  • Tree Information: Retained to maintain app functionality for the community
  • Reviews: Retained to preserve community content integrity
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated and anonymized after 24 months
  • Location Data: Only stored when you add trees; deleted upon account deletion

Children's Privacy

Scampr is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Tree climbing involves significant safety risks that require adult judgment and supervision. If we learn we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete that information promptly.

  • Update: Modify your account information and preferences
  • Delete: Delete your account and associated data

Privacy Settings

  • Location Services: Control location access in device settings
  • Notifications: Manage notification preferences in the app

Legal Rights (where applicable)

  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find nature objects near me?

Open the app and allow location access when prompted. Use the Map view to see nearby nature objects like trees, rocks, caves, waterfalls, and more. You can also use the Discover tab to search for specific types of nature.

How do I add a new nature object?

Tap the "+" button on the map to add a new nature object. You'll need to provide the location, photos, type (tree, rock, cave, etc.), and any additional details. Make sure you have permission to share locations on private property.

What types of nature can I add?

Scampr supports many types of nature objects including trees, rocks, caves, waterfalls, skies (scenic viewpoints), and plants. Each type has specific attributes you can describe.

Why isn't the app finding my location?

Make sure you've granted location permissions to Scampr in your device settings. If you're still having issues, try restarting the app or your device. Some indoor locations may have poor GPS signal.

How do I report inappropriate content?

If you see content that violates our community guidelines, please contact us at jordangillispie@outlook.com with details about the content.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes, you can delete your account through the Profile tab in the app. This will remove your personal information. Contact support if you need complete data removal.

The app is crashing or not working properly

Try these troubleshooting steps:

  • Force close and restart the app
  • Check for app updates in your app store
  • Restart your device
  • Make sure you have a stable internet connection
  • Free up device storage space

If problems persist, contact support with your device details.

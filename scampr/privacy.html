Introduction

At Scampr, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect several types of information from and about users of our App:

Data Type What We Collect Purpose
Account Information Email address, display name, phone number Account creation, authentication, communication
Nature Content Object locations, descriptions, photos, features App functionality, sharing with community
Reviews & Ratings Your reviews and comments about nature objects Community features
Location Data GPS coordinates (when you use location features) Finding nearby objects, location-based search
Device Information Device type, OS version, app version Technical support, bug fixes

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide Core Services: Enable nature discovery, mapping, and community features

  • Communication: Send important app updates and support messages
  • Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to enhance app functionality
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraudulent or harmful activities

Sharing of Information

Public Information

  • Reviews: Your reviews are public with your display name

  • Cloud hosting and database services (Firebase)
  • Map and location services (Google Maps)

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information including encryption, access control, and secure authentication systems.

Data Retention

  • Account Data: Until you delete your account

Children's Privacy

Scampr is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete that information promptly.
